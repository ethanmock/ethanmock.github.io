## Result provenance

- SIGN: arXiv v6, Table 2. Frappe AUC 0.9580; MovieLens-tag AUC 0.9407. The comparison bars reproduce xDeepFM, SIGN and L₀-SIGN Frappe AUC values (0.9435, 0.9448, 0.9580).
- IEDR: arXiv v2, Table 3. Frappe NDCG@10 74.11 versus strongest baseline 71.49 (3.66% relative improvement); Yelp NDCG@10 53.05 versus 50.94 (4.14% relative improvement).
- UniPMT: paper Figure 3 and “Clinical applications of P–M–T binding prediction.” 312 experimentally validated positive neoantigen triplets plus generated negatives; PR-AUC 72.15%, ROC-AUC 94.29%. These results concern binding prediction in the stated evaluation setting, not treatment outcomes.
